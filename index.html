About

I'm from Centerville, Utah, and I studied finance at BYU. During my undergrad I worked as a financial analyst co-op at IBM and a financial analyst intern at Target.

After graduating I joined Brex, a corporate spend management fintech, in an operations rotational program. I moved from analyst to associate manager. My biggest impacts came from partnering with engineering to fix issues customers experienced. I redesigned case workflows to cut case volume in half, automated 2,000+ wire return emails, and built country-specific solutions in product that dropped the international wire return rate by 47%. I also established internal policy to enable smoth customer deposits.

Before Brex hired a dedicated PM for our team, I was effectively doing the role by sitting with engineers, helping to prioritize what to build, and defending the tradeoffs. By the end of my time at Brex, I was managing a team of nine analysts and interns.

I then joined Cicero Group, a boutique consulting firm in Salt Lake City, to expand my strategic toolset. I ran comparable acquisition analysis for an M&A deal that closed $125M in acquisitions, and measured outcomes for a nonprofit serving roughly 17,000 high school seniors. Both taught me how much decision quality depends on the data underneath it.

The PM work at Brex ended up being what I looked forward to most. Operations taught me how to build with a customer focus, and how to lead a team. Finance gave me a profitability lens; consulting gave me rigor about data. I'm now at BYU's Marriott School for my MBA, working toward a product internship in summer 2027.
